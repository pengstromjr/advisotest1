# Compliance Notes

Adviso deals with academic planning and may eventually touch sensitive student information. Treat correctness, privacy, and user trust as core product requirements, not afterthoughts.

## Current Guardrails

- Do not claim to be an official UC Davis advisor unless approved.
- Distinguish official rules from likely interpretations and uncertain assumptions.
- Explain reasoning when students may act on advice.
- Be careful with transcript uploads and personally identifiable academic information.
- Avoid storing unnecessary sensitive student data.

## Open Questions

- What data is stored from uploaded transcripts?
- Are transcript uploads processed transiently or retained?
- What privacy policy/terms are needed before pilot use?
- What disclaimers are needed in-product?
- What institutional approvals would be needed for an official university partnership?

## Possible Product Requirements

- Visible disclaimer that Adviso is not official academic advice.
- Source links or citations for catalog/course requirements.
- Clear uncertainty language when requirements are ambiguous.
- Easy export/delete path for student-provided data.
