# User Research

Use this for notes from UC Davis students, advisors, pilots, interviews, and observed pain points.

## Target Initial User

UC Davis student who needs fast, personalized advising and academic planning support without waiting for a human advisor.

## Research Questions

- What advising questions do students need answered most urgently?
- What parts of schedule planning are most confusing?
- What makes students trust or distrust AI academic advice?
- Where do students currently get unofficial advice?
- What data or explanation would make an answer feel reliable?
